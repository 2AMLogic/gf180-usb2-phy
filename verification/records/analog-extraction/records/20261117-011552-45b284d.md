  measurement — the same silently-wrong failure shape the
  differential_receiver probe documented, so the trap's bite is not
  uniform across testbenches.

  The post-layout §6 finding on `differential_driver` is a property of the
  committed layout, not of the extraction: an LVS-level extraction of the
  same stream (`klt extract` without `--parasitics`, port-reordered the
  same way) reproduces the schematic timing (trise 5.82 ns / tfall 5.86 ns

  same corner localizes the entire slowdown to the series resistance on
  the two output nets `DP_OUTINT`/`DM_OUTINT` (per-net star totals
  439 Ω / 677 Ω; the arm feeding the `rm1` series resistor on `DP_OUTINT`

  stage and the resistor in the committed routing): with every parasitic R
  shrunk to 1 mΩ the edge returns to 6.29 ns; with only the output-path R
  restored it is back at 29.40 ns; gate-path-only and supply-only legs
  move nothing (6.31 ns / 29.40 ns). The devices are LVS-matched; the edge
  is slowed by the committed routing's own Metal1 resistance into the
  50 pF test load. See the driver grid record for the full-matrix verdict.
