- **Result**:
  - **What was wrong with the superseded evidence (measured).** The
    probe (`annotation-probe/`, OpenSTA on the same LEFs, DEF, liberty and
    clock) on record `20261008-200000-8139bb6`'s SPEF, at all three
    corners:
    - the timing paths before and after `read_spef` are byte-identical
      (`delay_changed: false` in the cited envelope);
    - `report_net _013_` (an ordinary two-pin net) shows wire capacitance
      0.0000 pF after `read_spef`, where the SPEF gives it 2.45 fF;
    - 1001 `STA-1656 pin … not found` warnings (OpenSTA stops printing at
      1000) on top of the 1652 `STA-1650` warnings the envelope reports.

    Root cause, from the SPEF text. Without `--def-pins`, the extractor
    declares every DEF-named net as a top-level port: all 343 nets appear
    in `*PORTS` and each has a `*P <net>` entry. It also writes the hub
    node of each net as the bare net name (`_013_`), not as an internal
    node (`_013_:1`). OpenSTA reads a bare name as a port pin and finds no
    such port, because the DEF has 37. It then drops every CAP and RES
    element that touches the hub, which is all of them, so no design net
    gets any wire parasitics. `report_parasitic_annotation` lists 307
    partially unannotated drivers. "342 of 342 design nets annotated"
    only counts net names that matched; it does not mean any parasitics
    were attached. The superseded records' statement that the incomplete
    annotation was only discarded physical-only nets, "not a correlation
    miss", was wrong. Every number in `20261008-200000-8139bb6` and
    `20260922-042422-9ec2304` is wire-parasitic-free timing (pin
    capacitance only), not a post-layout measurement. The superseded
    single-corner record's SPEF has the same structure (`*P _013_ B`, bare
    hub nodes).
  - **Repair.** The pinned klt has `--def-pins <DEF>`, which takes the
    declared port set from the DEF's own `PINS` section. With it, 306 nets
    stay internal (the extractor's warning) and their hubs become `net:1`.
    `*PORTS` has 37 entries. As a cross-check, a value-preserving rewrite
    of the old SPEF (`annotation-probe/repair_spef_hub_nodes.py`) gives the
    same worst setup and hold slack to four decimals at every corner (see
    the probe table). The rewrite renames bare hub nodes to `:1` and drops
    the false port entries; it changes no R or C value and is not cited
    evidence.
  - **Envelope (`sta-corners.json`)**: `status: "ok"`,
    `geometry_source: "routed"`, three distinct `corners`, each
    `timing_status: "constrained"`, 0 setup / 0 hold violations, TNS 0,
    `provenance.input.content_hash` = DEF `sha256:82a57e76…`. At every
    corner: **`delay_changed: true`**, `unannotated_driver_count` 7,
    `partially_unannotated_driver_count` 3, `design_nets_annotated` 342 of

    All three corners still meet setup and hold, with at least 30.9 ns of
    setup margin at the 12 MHz clock.
  - **What the remaining diagnostics are (measured, not assumed)**:
    - *7 unannotated drivers* = `clkload0/ZN` … `clkload6/ZN`. These are
      CTS dummy-load inverters whose output connects to no DEF net. The
      extractor emits their floating outputs as seven duplicate
      `*D_NET ZN` blocks, and OpenSTA rejects them (`STA-1650 net ZN`).
      No timing path goes through these outputs.
    - *3 partially unannotated drivers* = `_341_/Q`,
      `u_line_state/_6_/Q`, `u_line_state/_7_/Q`. They drive the three
      nets whose DEF PIN name differs from the DEF NET name: `RxActive`
      on net `rx_receiving`, and `LineState[0]` and `LineState[1]` on nets
      `line_state[0]` and `line_state[1]`. `--def-pins` keeps those nets
      as ports under the net name, so their hubs stay bare and OpenSTA
      drops them (`STA-1656`, 180 lines). Those three nets carry 0 wire
      capacitance in this run. With the repaired-hub cross-check, which
      attaches 28.9, 10.6 and 11.3 fF to them, worst setup and hold slack
      do not change in the fourth decimal at any corner. So the cited
      worst-slack figures do not depend on these three nets.
    - *643 `STA-1650 net $N not found`*: these are the extractor's
      unnamed nets. Every one of them is poly and `metal0` only (457 fF
      poly, 411 fF `metal0` in total) and ends on transistor terminals.
      They are standard-cell internal nets, which the liberty
      characterization already covers, not routing. Coupling from design
      nets to non-design nets totals 3.1 fF, against 1675 fF of grounded
      design-net capacitance; the largest single-net share is 0.53 fF.

- **Coverage gaps**:
  - *Wire resistance does not reach any pin-to-pin path.* This is how the
    extractor models resistance at the pin, not a reader problem. Every
    pin connects to its net's hub through a 0 Ω leg (1091 of 1091). The
    per-net resistance is a star from the hub to internal nodes that carry
    no capacitance and no pins (13 847 such nodes, 3 with any
    capacitance). OpenSTA therefore sees each net as a lumped capacitance.
    The `--distributed-rc --critical-net` mode, which would add
    resistance, was not used. The largest per-net resistance sum is
    7.3 kΩ on net `_053_`. As a rough, unmeasured bound, ΣR·(C_wire +
    100 fF) for that net is about 1.3 ns, which is well inside the
    30.9 ns worst-corner setup margin. Wire resistance only adds data-path
    delay under an ideal clock, so it cannot reduce hold slack. This
    estimate is not a measurement.
  - *Ideal clock.* `klt sta` times this post-CTS DEF with an ideal clock,
    so slack does not include clock-tree insertion delay. Skew is reported
    as a separate metric. This is a known tool gap (klayout-tools#2739).
  - *Lateral (same-layer) coupling* is only modelled for
    `--critical-net` nets. None were declared, so only vertical-overlap
    coupling is present.
  - Three liberty corners only. Spec §8.1 fixes a 45-corner matrix
    (3 T × 3 V × 5 process including `fs`/`sf`). This record does not
    claim it. There is no SDF gate-level run (#93). Analog PVT is
    untouched (klayout-tools#1962).
