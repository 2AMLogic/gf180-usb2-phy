## What is NOT established

The clean verdict is still narrower than a substrate-continuity proof. Each
of these is measured, not estimated.

- **Metal reach, not low impedance.** Every drawn pad now reaches the rail
  through metal, and this record still says nothing about the resistance of
  that path: no IR-drop, EM or latch-up number exists in this repo, and
  `klt power` was not run. The PDK's 20 um substrate-tap reach rule
  (DRM 7.5 Comp DF.13/DF.14) remains a DRC matter, passed by the deck.
- **`erc.supply_short` is not computed.** Unchanged from the superseded
  record: `substrate_continuity_not_modelled` in `erc_coverage.inapplicable`
  on every run; the cross-check is the LVS table above pairing the supplies
  as distinct nets at `match`.
- **The severed-rail half is not measured.** `nets[].roles` still not
  declared (supplies share every metal with signals);
  `power_delivery.supply_unlabelled_islands` remains `{}`, klt's honest
  "not measured".
- **No antenna verdict.** `--pdk` deliberately not passed; unchanged.
- **Nothing about the n-well tie's electrical quality.** More pads are
  wired; how far apart and what series resistance they present is not this
  check's subject.
- **The strap legs that were cut.** `_strap_body_pads` draws each leg only
  where it clears foreign metal-1; on these streams it drew 196 / 61 / 114 /
  58 / 58 leg pieces and dropped none, but the segments are cut where klt's
  routes cross the pad columns, so the metal path is a routed mesh of strap
  pieces and per-pad stubs, not an idealised ring. The census measures the
  result (every pad wired), not the shape.
- **The signoff manifest still grades one stream.** Item 11.analog cites the
  `(erc, lvs)` pair for `differential_driver`, the largest stream (51
  asserted polygons, 84 pads, all wired); the other four streams' evidence
  is this record, pinned by hash in `provenance.inputs[]`, and re-runnable
  with the harness whose self-test runs in CI.
