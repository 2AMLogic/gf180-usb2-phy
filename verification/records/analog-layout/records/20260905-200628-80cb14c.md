  sees them; and no `kdb.Polygon`/`kdb.DPolygon` construction exists anywhere
  in `gen_compose.py`. Those are specific, falsifiable claims about the
  source, and this record does not dispute them as *source-reading*
  findings — it has not re-read `gen_compose.py` itself. What this record
  *does* establish, independently and empirically, is that **the phenomenon

  the exact commit the source inspection covered**: a fully-unrouted net

  width-violating, tapered quadrilateral in the output GDS. The refutation
  closes the question "does `gen_compose.py` construct a mitered polygon

  "why does a width-violating tapered quadrilateral still appear at exactly
  a rejected net's unterminated end?" — that discrepancy is unresolved by
