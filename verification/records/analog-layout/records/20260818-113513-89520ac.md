  bbox"); a single drawing layer, so a multi-block `VDD`/`VSS`/bias bus

  every leg's geometry for that net. `layout/README.md` § "Analog" carries
  the full diagnosis and the three friction issues filed upstream
  (klayout-tools#1163, #1164, #1165).

  reported because it isolates the failure to routing rather than
