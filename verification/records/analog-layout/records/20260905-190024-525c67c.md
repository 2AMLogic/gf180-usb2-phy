  1. **`klt` now ingests `dplus_pullup`.** The direct probe returns a
     complete netlist digest: circuit `DPLUS_PULLUP`, **78 devices** (66
     `PFET`, 6 `NFET`, 6 `PPOLYF_U`) across **21 nets**, with every device's
     `W`/`L` resolved — the sixty flattened switch fingers each land as
     `W=100.0`, `L=0.28`. The full digest is this record's
     `dplus_pullup-netlist-digest.json` artifact. The superseded record's
     error at this exact call — "which klt's subckt-call → plain-element
     conversion refuses to represent" — no longer occurs. `scripts/
     gen_analog_layout.py` reaches the same conclusion through its own
     `_probe_blocked()` path and reports it in its own words: "klt now
     ingests this netlist -- the blocker this entry records is gone; author
     a plan under layout/analog/plans/ and drop this block from
     BLOCKED_BLOCKS".
  2. **The `klt layout-plan` executor places the flattened block.** Because
     no committed plan exists for `dplus_pullup` (authoring one is real
     per-block layout design work, deliberately out of this issue's scope —
     see "Not in scope" below), a **throwaway two-group probe plan** was
     driven through `execute_layout_plan_document` purely to prove the
     ingestion path is clear end to end rather than only at the digest
     stage. It is committed under `artifacts/` as evidence, **not** under
     `layout/analog/plans/`, because it is not a design. Result: both groups
     place — the six-resistor ladder at 76.12 × 4.00 µm and the ten
     flattened `MEN` fingers as a `mos_array` at 15.10 × 101.12 µm (ten
     100 µm-wide fingers, as drawn) — for an overall 93.22 × 101.12 µm
     bbox, and **1 of 8 nets routes**. The seven unrouted nets and the 38
     `metal1.width.1` violations in the probe GDS are the *same* router and
     rejected-leg-geometry limitations the superseded record already
     characterised for the three placeable blocks (upstream:
     [klayout-tools#1424](https://github.com/2AMLogic/klayout-tools/issues/1424));
     they are a plan-authoring and router problem, categorically not the
     ingestion blocker this record is about. The probe also emits five
     `length_um` disagreement warnings, which are an artifact of the probe
     lumping six different-length ladder resistors into one `res_array` —
     a defect of the throwaway probe, not of the design or of `klt`.
  3. **Nothing else moved.** `differential_driver`'s `rm1` (2-terminal metal
     resistor) blocker is byte-for-byte unchanged, as expected: the
     `device_map` capability that landed upstream is documented as
     MOS-shaped-only. The three placeable blocks are byte-identical, per the
     GDS comparison above. The aggregate verdict line the driver prints is
     unchanged: `analog layout NOT delivered`.
