# Record 20261010-120000-cc4e89c

- **Record ID**: 20261010-120000-cc4e89c
- **Claim**: the UTMI wrapper's new driver-enable output `txoe`
  (issue #113; pin contract `spec/decisions/0004`) is high for exactly the
  states where the wrapper drives the line (`tx_state != TX_IDLE`) and low
  otherwise. This record also covers everything `20261009-141010-5e89df9` claimed (the
  framing, line-state, sync/EOP and top-level suites incl. UTMI Reset in
  TX), re-run with unchanged assertions on the changed RTL. New module
  `verification/test_usb_utmi_txoe.py` (RTL-only; deliberately separate
  from `test_usb_utmi_phy.py`, which is also replayed against the
  committed routed netlist that predates `txoe`) has 5 tests, each
  sampling `txoe` at the same falling edge as `txdp`/`txdm`:
  1. `test_txoe_idle_and_rx_low`: low after reset, in idle, and
     throughout a received frame (RxActive seen high).
  2. `test_txoe_spans_packet_sync_through_final_j`: one pulse; the first
     high sample carries SYNC bit 0 (K) after idle J; the last high
     sample is the J of SE0,SE0,J; the next sample is idle J with `txoe`
     low.
  3. `test_txoe_high_through_flush_path`: payload `[0x00, 0xFC]` (TX_FLUSH
     path): a single uninterrupted pulse, same boundaries.
  4. `test_txoe_raw_mode`: OpMode 2'b10, same boundaries.
  5. `test_txoe_back_to_back_packets`: actual behaviour asserted, not
     assumed: two packets give two separate pulses with a low gap of at
     least one clock (the second packet starts only from TX_IDLE).
