# Record 20261010-052224-9847dbc

- **Record ID**: 20261010-052224-9847dbc
- **Claim**: UTMI `Reset` reaches the vendored RX transforms
  (`usb_nrzi_decoder`, `usb_bit_destuffer`) synchronously (issue #132,
  `spec/decisions/0006`). Their asynchronous reset port now takes only
  `rst_n`. On every edge that samples `Reset` high, canonical inputs load
  the decoder's idle-J reference and clear the destuffer's run, forwarded
  bit and error. One wrapper flop (`rx_dec_mask`) hides the decoder's
  reset-edge strobe from the SYNC search and the destuffer for the one
  clock after. The receive contract under `Reset` is now verified at the
  ports by 52 new cases in `verification/test_usb_utmi_phy.py`:
  1. `test_rx_utmi_reset_edge_timed_abort_and_release` (40 cases: 10
     model-located phases x hold 1/3 x release J/K; the cocotb names number
     the phases in `RX_RESET_PHASES` order: sync, sync_final, first_bit,
     partial_byte, byte_complete, six_ones, stuff_bit, error, error_out,
     eop). The RX ports do not change 1 ns after `Reset` rises or falls,
     nor shortly before the next rising edge. The sampling edge suppresses
     the pending byte (byte_complete) or error (error, error_out).
     `RxActive`/`RxValid`/`RxError` stay low on every reset clock, and
     `DataIn` does not change. The aborted tail is ignored after a J or a K
     release. A fresh packet (sixteen leading 1s, two stuffed 0s, a
     trailing stuff bit) is exact. The RTL run also checks the internal
     state through hierarchical names: decoder `prev_level` = J, destuffer
     run/`bit_valid`/`stuff_err` = 0 after each reset edge, and SYNC search
     empty after the release edge. The destuffer run at injection (5 or 6)
     confirms the stuffing boundary.
  2. `test_rx_utmi_reset_release_straight_into_sync` (8 cases: J or K
     before reset x hold 1/3 x SYNC starting on the release edge or one
     cell later). Two payloads are received exactly, with no error.
  3. `test_rx_utmi_reset_pulse_between_edges_is_not_sampled` (4 cases;
     phases sync, partial_byte, six_ones, stuff_bit). This is a structural
     probe, not a UTMI-legal stimulus: a `Reset` pulse that no edge samples
     leaves the packet in flight exact.
  This record also covers everything `20261010-120000-cc4e89c` claimed
  (line-state, sync, EOP, the top-level suite including TX reset and
  `pu_en`, and `txoe`), re-run with unchanged assertions on the changed
  RTL.
