# Record 20261008-235500-9e3008a

- **Record ID**: 20261008-235500-9e3008a
- **Claim**: top-level RX stuffing-error propagation and recovery of
  `usb_utmi_phy` (issue #118), in addition to everything record
  `20261008-212139-afadb58` claimed (the framing/line-state/top-level suite,
  re-run here with unchanged assertions). Six new directed tests in
  `verification/test_usb_utmi_phy.py` drive the receive pins from the
  bit-level model (independent wire stimulus, not TX loopback, which can
  only produce correctly stuffed frames): (1) control -- correctly stuffed
  wire packets, including a mid-byte stuffed 0 and the #7.1.9 trailing
  stuffed 0, yield exact bytes and no `RxError`; (2) the stuffed 0 deleted
  from inside a byte (seven consecutive 1s while `RxActive` is high) gives
  exactly one `RxError` pulse during active reception, before EOP, at a
  fixed 3-clock latency from the violating wire cell (observed; asserted
  only as 0..4 clocks), `RxActive` falls within 6 clocks of the first SE0
  cell, and the next clean packet delivers exact bytes with no inherited
  error; (3) the same with the trailing stuffed 0 replaced by a 1 directly
  before EOP; (4) a run of idle-J 1s with no SYNC raises no `RxError`/
  `RxActive`; (5) UTMI `Reset` asserted mid-packet with a partial byte
  assembled clears `RxActive`, leaks no `RxValid`/`DataIn`/`RxError`, the
  aborted packet's remaining wire cells are ignored, and the next clean
  packet is exact; (6) `Reset` after a stuffing error inside the same packet
  leaves no error/byte state. No new error semantics are asserted: partial
  byte and SE1 behavior are not claimed, and the bytes of a violated packet
  are not checked (only its error, termination, and the recovery after it).
