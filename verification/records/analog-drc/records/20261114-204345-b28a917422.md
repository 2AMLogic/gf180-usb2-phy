### What the coverage block says, in prose

- **This is klt's curated rule set, not the foundry signoff deck.**
  `coverage.deck_scope` above is the deck's own account of what it covers:
  design rules drawn from specific sections of the foundry manual (`7.4`
  Nwell through `9.1` Bond Pad) plus EF/ESD/GR/MDN/MDP/OFFGRID/SB. It is not
  the foundry's signoff deck, and a `clean` here is not a foundry signoff.
- **`clean` is not a density-clean claim.** The deck has no density rules, so
  no metal-fill requirement is checked and none is inserted. Same caveat as
  the `digital-drc` record.
- **68-69 rules are skipped per stream** — quoted in full above, roughly half
  the deck's rule set. The skipped lists include the multi-voltage rules
  (`DF.*_MV`, listed in `coverage.unmodeled_rules`) and `resistor.ongrid.1`
  on the two streams that do not trigger it.
- **Every stream carries layers with no rules**: `34/10` on all five, plus
  `110/11` on `differential_driver`. Geometry on those layers is present in
  the stream and unchecked by this deck.
- **`coverage.unmodeled_rules` holds 210 entries on every stream** — larger
  than the deck's entire checked-plus-skipped set (126 rules per stream).
  These are foundry rules the curated deck does not model at all: they are
  neither checked nor skipped-and-disclosed, they are simply outside this
  deck's rule set.
- The five envelopes also disclose, and this record carries over:
  `coverage.nothing_checked: false` (the deck did check these streams),
  `coverage.voltage_domain_warnings: []`, `coverage.unchecked_rules: []`,
  `coverage.marker_blind_layers_in_stream: []`, and
  `coverage.inapplicable`: **69 entries** on `differential_driver` and
  `dplus_pullup`, **68** on `differential_receiver`, `se_receiver_dm` and
  `se_receiver_dp` — on every stream exactly the rule ids of the
  `rules_skipped` lists quoted in full above (the 69-entry list is the
  68-entry list plus `resistor.ongrid.1`), but carried as objects with a
  reason: `no_applicable_geometry` (67 rules on the two 69-entry streams,
  66 on the three 68-entry ones) and `inapplicable_empty_checked_region`
  (2 rules on every stream).

### The klayout version mismatch, disclosed plainly

`provenance.klayout_version_mismatch` is `true` on **all five** envelopes:
the resolved klayout engine is `0.30.13`, while klt `0.9.0` was built and
tested against `klayout==0.30.12` (klt prints this warning itself and states
the verdict is unaffected; the same pairing is quoted in the `analog-lvs`
record and used by every record minted at this pin). **Does it weaken this
claim?** It does not change what the deck contains — the deck's identity is
its content hash, quoted above, and the skipped/checked lists are disclosed
from the same envelopes — but it is a real deviation from the tested pairing,
and a different engine version could in principle evaluate a rule
differently. The claim is therefore "clean under deck
`cf44de63...` as evaluated by klayout 0.30.13 through klt 0.9.0", not
"clean under any engine"; if the pin or engine moves, the append-only rule
applies and a fresh record is minted.
