**The one non-identity, stated rather than smoothed over.** The two forms are
not textually identical in their junction-parasitic parameters. The PDK
symbol's template expressions compute drain/source diffusion area and
perimeter from the finger count, so a shared-diffusion `nf=10` device claims
`ad = 5 * (W/nf) * 0.18u` where ten isolated one-finger devices claim
`10 * 1 * (W/nf) * 0.18u` -- roughly 2x the drain area and 1.7x the source
area in total, because ten separately drawn devices do not share diffusions
the way an interdigitated finger array does. That difference is *purely
capacitive*; this record's measurement is a DC sweep, so it cannot affect any
number in the table above. The DC-relevant parameters are unchanged in
aggregate: `nrd = nrs = 0.18u/W` gives 1.8e-4 squares for the `nf=10` device
and 1.8e-3 squares for each of ten parallel one-finger devices, whose
parallel combination is the same 1.8e-4; and BSIM4 derives its per-finger
effective width as `W/NF`, which is 100 um in both forms. The residual ~1e-5
relative deviations above are the expected floating-point consequence of
